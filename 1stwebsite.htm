Manali

Manali is a resort town nestled in the mountains of Himachal Pradesh, India. It sits near the northern end of the Kullu Valley at an altitude of 2,050 meters. The town is famous for its snow-capped peaks, thick pine forests, and the Beas River. It serves as a popular destination for backpacking, honeymooning, and adventure sports like skiing, paragliding, and trekking. Additionally, Manali acts as the gateway to the Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, and the remote regions of Lahaul and Spiti.

About Manali

Everything you need to know about this paradise

  • Manali is a beautiful town located in the Kullu Valley of Himachal Pradesh, India. Situated at an altitude of about 2,050 meters (6,726 feet) above sea level, it is surrounded by the majestic Himalayas, dense forests, and the Beas River.

  • Manali is not just a tourist destination — it's an experience! Whether you're looking for thrilling adventure sports like paragliding and rafting, or peaceful moments by snow-covered peaks and apple orchards, Manali has it all.

  • The town is also known as the adventure capital of India and is a gateway to famous destinations like Rohtang Pass, Solang Valley, and Atal Bihari Vajpayee Mountaineering Institute.

Popular Places

Must-visit destinations in and around Manali

1. Solang Valley

A stunning valley known for adventure sports like paragliding, zip-lining, and skiing. Offers breathtaking views of snow-capped peaks all year round.

2. Rohtang Pass

A high mountain pass at 3,978 meters offering spectacular views, snow experiences, and access to pristine landscapes. A must-visit for nature lovers!

3. Hidimba Devi Temple

A historic 15th-century temple dedicated to Goddess Hidimba, featuring unique architecture with a tall wooden spire and beautiful surroundings.


Best Time to Visit

Manali is beautiful all year round — choose your season!

Season/Months Temperature Highlights
🌸 March-June 10°C — 25°C Perfect weather for sightseeing, adventure sports, and exploring. The best time to visit! Flowers bloom and everything is green.
☀️ July - August 15°C — 22°C Monsoon season with lush greenery and waterfalls at their best. But be careful — landslides can happen. Great for peaceful visits!
🍂 September - October 10°C — 20°C Post-monsoon magic! Crisp air, clear skies, and beautiful autumn colors. Perfect for photography and trekking.
❄️ November - February -1°C — 12°C Winter wonderland! Snowfalls, freezing temperatures, and a magical white landscape. Ideal for snow lovers and winter sports enthusiasts.

Things to Do

Adventure, fun, and unforgettable experiences await!

🪂 Paragliding

Soar above the valleys and experience breathtaking views from the sky. Available in Solang Valley!

🏄 River Rafting

Feel the adrenaline rush as you navigate the rapids of the Beas River. Fun for all skill levels!

⛷️ Skiing

Hit the slopes at Solang Valley and Rohtang Pass. Winter sports paradise with stunning snow!

🚴 Mountain Biking

Explore winding mountain roads and trails on a bike. A thrilling way to see the countryside!


Festivals & Culture

Experience the vibrant culture and celebrations of Manali 🎭

🎭 Hadimba Devi Festival (May)

One of the most famous festivals in Himachal! A week-long extravaganza with thousands of deities paraded, cultural performances, and vibrant energy.

🎉 Kullu Dussehra (October)

Celebrated in the Kullu valley, this festival brings joy, beautiful local dances, and great local food to try.

🍎 Apple Harvest Festival (September)

Celebrate the bounty of apple orchards! Enjoy fresh apples, local crafts, folk music, and the beautiful autumn scenery of Manali's orchards.
